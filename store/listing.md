## Description
WinZones is a fast, free window manager for Windows 10 and 11.

Hold Shift while dragging any window and a frosted-glass overlay shows your zones. Drop the window and it slides into place. Add Ctrl to span several zones at once.

Too many windows open? Press Win + Alt + A, or drop a window on the "Auto-arrange" pill, and WinZones tiles everything at once. Editors and browsers get the big columns, while chat, music and terminals stack neatly at the side. It works across every monitor and shines on ultrawide screens.

• Your own layouts: draw them in a visual editor, one per monitor
• Global shortcuts for snapping, cycling zones, switching layouts, fill, center and moving to the next monitor
• Command menu: right-click the tray icon, type a few letters and press Enter
• Multi-level undo with Win + Alt + Z
• Smooth animations that stay light even on 5K ultrawide monitors
• A clean, dark settings app where every shortcut can be rebound
• Private by design: no account, no ads, no tracking, no data collection

WinZones is open source (MIT License): https://github.com/Coursion-Studio/WinZones
